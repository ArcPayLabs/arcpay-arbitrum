## Demo Flow

1. Open the live app and connect an Arbitrum Sepolia wallet.
2. Show dashboard status and deployed contract map.
3. Open Agents and show ERC-8004 identity/onboarding.
4. Open Policies and show spend/action controls.
5. Open x402 and show quote, payment requirement, verify, and unlock.
6. Open Execution and show GMX/ZeroDev/Dune/Fhenix evidence surfaces.
7. Open Cards and show USDC agent card flow.
8. Open Privacy, Invoices, Audit, and Status to show proof capture.
